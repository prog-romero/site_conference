Code of Conduct

Principles and rules that guide our community during the conference

Our Commitment

The Cameroon-France Artificial Intelligence Conference 2025 is committed to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

We do not tolerate any form of harassment or discrimination among participants, speakers, sponsors, organizers, volunteers, and staff. All attendees of this event must adhere to this code of conduct for the entire duration of the event in all conference-related venues and communication channels.

Objectives of this Code of Conduct

  • Foster an inclusive and respectful environment
  • encourage participation and collaboration between different cultures and communities
  • Promote the exchange of ideas and diversity of perspectives
  • Ensure the safety and well-being of all participants

Expected Behaviors

We expect all participants to:

  • Show respect and courtesy towards other participants
  • Communicate in a professional and constructive manner
  • Be attentive to speakers and other participants
  • Respect different perspectives and experiences
  • Focus on what is best for the community as a whole
  • Show empathy towards other community members
  • Respect the confidentiality of information shared during sessions

Unacceptable Behaviors

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable:

  • Any form of harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
  • Use of sexual, violent, or discriminatory language or imagery
  • Insults or derogatory remarks regarding gender, sexual orientation, religion, ethnicity, etc.
  • Intimidation or harassment of an individual, physically or verbally
  • Deliberate disruption of presentations or other events
  • Advocacy for or encouragement of any of the above behaviors
  • Inappropriate or disruptive behavior during virtual or hybrid sessions
  • Recording or photography without consent

Intercultural Specifics

As this conference is a collaboration between Cameroon and France, we are aware of the cultural differences that may exist. We encourage all participants to:

  • Be mindful and respectful of cultural differences
  • Avoid generalizations and cultural stereotypes
  • Be open-minded to different approaches and perspectives
  • Consider potential language barriers in communications

Incident Reporting Procedure

If you are a victim or witness of unacceptable behavior:

  1. Immediately report the incident to the organizing team or security personnel
  2. A dedicated team will be available to assist you and ensure your safety
  3. All complaints will be reviewed and handled promptly and fairly
  4. Confidentiality will be maintained to the extent possible

Reporting Contacts

To report an incident or concern, you can:

  • Contact a member of the organizing team directly (identifiable by their badge)
  • Send an email to conduct@ia-conference-cam-fr.org
  • Call the conference emergency number: +(237) 621 09 53 68
  • Use the conference app to anonymously report an incident

Consequences

The conference organizers will take appropriate action they deem necessary in response to any unacceptable behavior. These measures may include:

  • A verbal or written warning
  • Expulsion from the conference without a refund
  • Exclusion from future conferences or events
  • Reporting to local authorities if necessary

For Presenters and Speakers

Presenters are also required to adhere to this code of conduct. In particular:

  • Presentations must not contain offensive or inappropriate content
  • Presenters should be sensitive to the diversity of the audience
  • Criticism should be constructive and professional
  • Presenters must respect their allotted time

Virtual Environment

For the virtual or hybrid parts of our conference:

  • All aspects of this code of conduct also apply to online sessions
  • Unauthorized sharing of connection links is prohibited
  • Recording or screen capturing without permission is prohibited
  • Spam or off-topic messages in chats are discouraged

Note on Application

This code of conduct applies to all conference-related spaces, including conference sessions, workshops, social events, online platforms, social media, and communication channels using official event hashtags.

Acknowledgments

This code of conduct is inspired by the best practices of international technology conferences and adapted to the specific context of our Cameroon-France collaboration. We thank all participants for their cooperation in creating a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment.

Last updated: May 16, 2025

For any questions regarding this code of conduct, please contact conduct@ia-conference-cam-fr.org
